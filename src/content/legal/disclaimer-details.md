## What we do

Savantho provides financial consultancy and financial education to families, professionals, businesses and organisations in Kenya. That means planning, guidance and training: helping you understand your money, your options and the risks attached to each, so that you can make informed decisions.

Our services include wealth planning consultancy, Shariah-compliant finance guidance, real estate due diligence and structuring, trade finance consultancy, financial literacy training, and project monitoring and evaluation.

## What we do not do

- We are **not licensed by the Capital Markets Authority** (CMA), and we do not carry out activities that require a CMA licence.
- We **do not hold, manage or invest client funds**, and we never ask you to send money to us for investment.
- We **do not recommend specific securities**, such as particular shares, bonds, unit trusts or funds.
- We are **not a bank, lender, insurer or estate agent**. In real estate we act for buyers on due diligence and structuring; we do not sell or market property.
- We **do not issue Shariah rulings**. Our Shariah-compliant finance guidance explains how structures work and how they are screened; questions of religious ruling are for qualified scholars.

## Where regulated advice is needed

Some decisions need advice from a licensed professional. Where you need regulated investment advice, we refer you to an adviser licensed by the Capital Markets Authority. Where you need legal, tax or accounting advice, we refer you to, or work alongside, your advocate, tax adviser or accountant. If we receive anything for a referral, we tell you in writing first.

## Risk

Every financial decision carries risk. The value of assets can fall as well as rise, and past results say nothing certain about the future. Our role is to help you understand the risks before you commit; the decision, and its outcome, stay yours.

## Information on this website

Articles, guides and answers on this website are general information and financial education. They do not consider your personal circumstances and are not a recommendation to act. Rules, rates and product terms change; check the current position, and speak to us or a qualified professional, before you rely on anything here.

## Client stories

Where we publish client stories, they are shared with the client's written consent and describe the process and the experience of working with us. They never describe investment returns or performance.
