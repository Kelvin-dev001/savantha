faqs:
  - q: Are you an estate agent?
    a: >-
      No. We do not sell, market or broker property, and we do not act for sellers or
      developers. We work only for the buyer: checking what you are buying, working out the full
      cost, and helping you decide how to pay for it and hold it. That independence is the
      point of the service.
  - q: What does property due diligence involve in Kenya?
    a: >-
      It means confirming the seller owns what they are selling and that nothing stands in the
      way of a clean transfer. That covers the official search, the title and its history,
      charges and cautions, rates and rent clearance, approvals, access and boundaries. Your
      advocate handles the legal steps; we keep the whole file in order.
  - q: Can you help me buy property from abroad?
    a: >-
      Yes. Buying at a distance is where checks matter most, because you depend on people you
      cannot always see. We keep the due diligence file, explain every document as it arrives,
      and make sure the money moves only once the checks are complete and your advocate is
      satisfied.
  - q: Should I buy off-plan?
    a: >-
      It depends on the developer, the contract and your ability to wait. Off-plan can cost less,
      but you carry the risk of delay or non-completion. We review the developer's record, the
      approvals, the payment schedule and the contract terms with your advocate, so you
      understand those risks before you commit.
  - q: Do you value the property?
    a: >-
      No. Valuation is the work of a registered valuer, and your lender will usually insist on
      one. We tell you when a valuation is needed, what it should cover and how to read it. We
      then use it, with the other findings, in our written summary of the purchase.
